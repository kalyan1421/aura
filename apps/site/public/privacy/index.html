Privacy policy

Effective 25 September 2026 · Applies to the Aura app on iOS and Android and to this website.

Aura is built so that how you feel stays yours. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the service, how long we keep things and how you stay in control.

1. Who we are

Aura is published by AKVega, the data fiduciary for the personal data described here under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Questions, requests and complaints go to our Grievance Officer, Kalyan Kumar Bedugam, at akprojects1431@gmail.com (see Support).

3. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or show ads.
  • We don't use your moods, notes or voice to train AI models.
  • We don't run analytics that track you across other apps or sites.
  • We don't send notes, reflections, capsule content or your date of birth to analytics or logs.

4. Who helps us run Aura

These service providers process data only on our instructions, under contract, and only for the purpose shown:

  • Supabase and DigitalOcean: database, storage and servers.
  • OpenAI: automated safety checks on short room reflections, with no data retention for training. Private notes are never sent.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service: to deliver notifications you turned on. Notifications never say how you feel.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in (if you choose it) and purchases.
  • Our email provider: sign-in codes and service emails.

Some providers may process data outside India. When they do, we rely on contractual safeguards and only transfer data where the law allows.

5. How long we keep data

  • While your account exists, we keep your data so the app works.
  • When you delete your account, you have 7 days to change your mind. After that, your personal data, including check-ins, notes, voice, capsules, photos, habits, room activity, keys and your date of birth, is permanently deleted, always within 30 days of your request.
  • Some records are kept longer only where the law requires: purchase and tax records (without the link to your account), and evidence about reported content kept for up to 180 days for safety and legal reasons.
  • Rooms become read-only memories after a day and are removed after 7 days.

6. Your rights and choices

You can see and edit your check-ins in the app, turn notifications and features on or off in Settings, and delete your account at any time (Settings → Account → Delete my account, or on the web). You may also ask us for a summary of your data, a correction, deletion, or to name someone to act for you. Write to the Grievance Officer; we acknowledge within 24 hours and resolve within 7 days. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Young people

Aura is for people aged 13 and older. We block sign-ups under 13, and people under 18 get extra, invisible protections: stricter moderation and no analytics. If you believe someone under 13 is using Aura, tell us at akprojects1431@gmail.com. See our child safety standards.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, private content is encrypted at rest with per-account keys, the app's local database is encrypted, and staff access is limited, logged and protected with two-factor sign-in.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the current version.
