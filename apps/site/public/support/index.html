Common questions

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete my account. Or see delete your account.

How do I cancel Aura Plus?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Aura. On Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Aura. Deleting your Aura account doesn't cancel a store subscription.

Can other people see how I feel?

No. Your check-ins, notes and voice are private. In a room, others see only a fresh name for you and the gestures you send.
