Aura is a calm, pseudonymous mood check-in app. Tell Aura how you feel in two taps, watch your own celestial Aura grow, and sit quietly with others who feel the same tonight.

No real name. No DMs. No photos. No follower counts.

CHECK IN IN SECONDS
• Pick one of 16 everyday feelings. Add a tag or a private note if you like.
• Works offline. Your notes and voice notes are encrypted.

AN AURA THAT GROWS WITH YOU
• A glowing avatar that changes as you look after yourself.
• It never dies and never goes backwards. On hard days it rests in a soft nebula.
• A glow streak with rest days. Missing a day costs you nothing.

MOOD ROOMS
• A 24-hour room with people who feel the same, matched by mood, language and your evening.
• Send silent gestures: a cup of chai, a candle, "me too".
• Short reflections are checked before anyone sees them. There are no replies and no private messages.
• A fresh pseudonym in every room. Report, mute and block are always one tap away.
• Lantern Hour: your evening, when the rooms glow a little brighter.

BREATHE TOGETHER
• Breath Ripples: a 3-minute shared breathing game. No score, and no way to fail.
• Quiet Together: study, read or rest alongside others. Just a count, a shared sound and gestures.

A SANCTUARY JUST FOR YOU
• A breathing pacer with gentle haptics, grounding exercises and Today's minute.
• Soundscapes that work offline, and a night wind-down.

LETTERS TO YOUR FUTURE SELF
• Time capsules that open on a date, or the next time you feel a certain way.

SEE YOUR MONTHS
• Aura Sky shows your days as stars. Look back on your own history, collect keepsakes, and share a card of your Aura if you want to. Cards never show how you feel.
• A home-screen widget for a two-tap check-in.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
• English, తెలుగు and Tenglish.

SAFETY
• "I need help now" is on every main screen, works offline and shows helplines for your region.
• Aura is for everyone aged 13 and over.

PRIVACY
• No ads. No tracking. Your notes, voice notes and capsules are encrypted, and you can delete your account inside the app.

Aura is not a medical device or an emergency service. If you are in danger, please call your local emergency number.
