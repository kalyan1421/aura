Aura ♥ I need help now

A quiet place to notice how you feel.

Aura is a private mood check-in for iPhone and Android. Check in with one tap, watch your Aura glow as you show up for yourself, and, when you want company, sit for a while in a small room with people who feel the same way right now. No feeds, no followers, no messages.

Private by default

Your check-ins and notes are yours. Notes, voice notes and capsules are encrypted, and nobody sees how you feel unless you choose to share it.

Gentle rooms

A fresh name in every room, and warm gestures instead of messages. Rooms last a day, then rest as a memory.

Calm tools

Breathe, grounding, sounds and a minute for you. Nothing here is scored.

Future you

Seal a note for a date, or for a feeling, and let past you look after future you.

Wellness support, not therapy

Aura helps you notice how you feel. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything, and it isn't an emergency service. If you are in danger right now, call your local emergency number (112 in India) or see help now.

Aura is for people aged 13 and older. It is published by AKVega.
